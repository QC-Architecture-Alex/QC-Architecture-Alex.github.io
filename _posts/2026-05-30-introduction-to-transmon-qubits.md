# Introduction

Most superconducting quantum processors today are built using **transmon qubits**.

The transmon is an evolution of the Cooper-pair box that significantly reduces sensitivity to charge noise while preserving quantum behavior.

## Why are transmons popular?

Several factors contributed to their success:

- Relatively simple fabrication
- Long coherence times
- Compatibility with microwave control
- Easy integration into larger quantum processors

## Basic structure

A transmon consists of:

1. A capacitor
2. A Josephson junction

Together they create an anharmonic oscillator whose lowest two energy levels form a qubit.

## Challenges

Although transmons are currently the dominant technology, they still face challenges:

- Decoherence
- Crosstalk
- Scaling to millions of physical qubits

These challenges motivate research into error correction and alternative qubit technologies.

## Conclusion

Transmon qubits remain the foundation of most modern superconducting quantum computers and are likely to play a major role in fault-tolerant quantum computing over the coming years.
